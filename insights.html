Insights

This page shares how I think about solving problems, not just what I built. It is intentionally different from the home page and project list.

Decision Framework: Speed vs Certainty

For route search, Dijkstra guarantees the shortest path while A* often reaches practical answers faster. I use both depending on whether the context values optimal proof or fast user-facing response.

Why Monte Carlo Matters

Real systems behave under uncertainty. Monte Carlo simulation helped model demand volatility, estimate queue risk, and expose edge cases before implementation.

What Experience Taught Me

CFBL, Dojo, and WalkUp taught me to tie technical outputs to outcomes. The best solution is the one users can understand, trust, and adopt.

Building as a Graduate

I focus on clean fundamentals: reliable architecture, readable code, responsive interfaces, and measurable improvements. That keeps projects interview-ready and production-ready.
